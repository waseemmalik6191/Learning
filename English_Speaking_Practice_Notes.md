## 1. Talking About Cold and Warm Weather 🌦️

### My preferred weather

To be honest, I'm not a big fan of cold weather. I prefer warm weather, ideally between 25 and 30 degrees. The main reason is that cold weather makes me feel uncomfortable.

I don't like wearing multiple layers, like sweaters and gloves, just to keep warm. In winter, I need to be extra careful to stay warm, so I end up drinking more hot tea, eating hot food, and wrapping up in warm clothes.

On the other hand, warm weather is much more enjoyable. I can wear light clothes, enjoy cold drinks, eat ice cream, and spend time outdoors without worrying about the cold.

### Useful expressions

- To be honest, I'm not a big fan of...
- I prefer warm weather.
- The main reason is that...
- On the other hand...
- I end up doing...
- Without worrying about...

## 2. Talking About Soft Drinks 🥤

Actually, I love soft drinks. I used to drink them almost every day back in India, but I've stopped since moving to Ireland because of the cold weather.

Honestly, I haven't had any soft drinks since moving to Ireland. The weather is quite cold here, and I feel that drinking cold drinks makes me feel unwell. It also gives me a sore throat.

### Useful expressions

- I used to drink them almost every day.
- I've stopped since moving to Ireland.
- I haven't had any soft drinks since...
- It makes me feel unwell.
- It gives me a sore throat.

### Grammar tip: “Used to” vs. “Haven't”

**I used to drink soft drinks.**  
A past habit that is no longer true.

**I haven't had soft drinks since moving to Ireland.**  
Something that has not happened from the past until now.

## 7. Important Grammar Patterns to Remember ⭐

| Incorrect ❌ | Correct ✅ |
|---|---|
| I went Primark. | I went to Primark. |
| I went grocery shop. | I went grocery shopping. |
| I have lot on this week. | I have a lot on this week. |
| I have been busy in this week. | I've been busy this week. |
| I'm taking rest day. | I'm taking a rest day. |
| I did shopping. | I did some shopping. |
| I enjoy to cook. | I enjoy cooking. |
| In weekend. | At the weekend. / On the weekend. |

## 9. Daily English Speaking Practice Routine 🗣️

1. **Speak:** Say the sentence aloud without looking at the notes.
2. **Correct:** Get feedback on grammar, pronunciation, and word choice.
3. **Repeat:** Repeat the corrected sentence two or three times.
4. **Recap:** At the next practice session, revisit the previous sentences before learning new ones.

### How to use these notes

- Practice one topic each day.
- Don't try to memorize every word at once.
- Focus on speaking clearly rather than speaking quickly.
- Repeat sentences until they feel natural.
- Use the same expressions in real conversations.

**Your goal:** Speak naturally, confidently, and clearly — one sentence at a time.
