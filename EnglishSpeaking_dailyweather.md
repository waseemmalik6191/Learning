
# English Speaking Practice – Daily Conversations

A collection of everyday English phrases, natural expressions, and conversation examples to improve fluency and confidence.

---

## 1. Talking About the Weekend and Weather

### Weekend Summary

The weather was pretty bad all weekend. It was raining and windy, so I didn't get outside much. I caught up on some housework and went grocery shopping.

I spent most of my time cooking, doing the laundry, and watching TV. I actually enjoyed cooking.

I've got some leftover curry and roti from last night.

### Talking About the Weather

- I'm not a big fan of this weather.
- Hopefully, it'll be sunny tomorrow.
- Morning! Looks like it's lashing rain out there.
- Good morning! It looks like it's raining heavily outside.

### Useful Expressions

| Expression | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pretty bad | Quite bad |
| Catch up on housework | Complete household tasks you've postponed |
| Grocery shopping | Buying food and household essentials |
| Leftovers | Food remaining from a previous meal |
| Lashing rain | Raining very heavily (common Irish expression) |

## 2. Talking About My Gym Routine

I usually go to the gym in the morning. If I'm busy, I'll go in the evening.

I usually do push-ups and some cardio exercises.

### Alternative – More Natural English

"I usually work out in the morning, but if I'm busy, I go in the evening. My routine mainly includes push-ups and cardio."

### Useful Vocabulary

- Work out – Exercise.
- Cardio – Exercises that improve cardiovascular fitness.
- Routine – Something you regularly do.

## 3. Enquiring About School Enrolment

### Starting a Conversation

"Good morning. I'd like to enquire about enrolling my son. He's three years old."

### When Would You Like to Enrol?

"Ideally, as soon as possible."

### Asking About the Application Process

"Not yet. Could you please explain how the application process works?"

### Asking About School Timings

"Could you please tell me what time the school starts and finishes?"

### Useful Expressions

- I'd like to enquire about...
- Could you please explain...?
- Ideally, as soon as possible.
- What documents do I need to provide?
- Are there any places available?

## 4. Talking About Family

"I usually call my family or catch up with them at the weekend."

### Alternative Expressions

- I usually catch up with my family over the weekend.
- I spend some time talking to my family.
- I usually have a video call with my wife and son.

## 5. Talking About Cricket

"My favourite player is Virat Kohli. He's a fantastic batter, very consistent, and has a great passion for the game."

"I usually watch the big tournaments."

### Combined Conversation

"My favourite player is Virat Kohli. He's a fantastic batter, very consistent, and has a great passion for the game. I usually watch the big tournaments whenever I get time."

### Useful Vocabulary

- Batter – A player who bats in cricket.
- Consistent – Performing well regularly.
- Passion – A strong interest or enthusiasm.
- Tournament – A series of competitive matches.

## 6. Friendly Small Talk

### Question: How are they?

Answer:
"They're good, thanks."

### Question: How's your little one?

Answer:
"He's doing well, thanks. He keeps me busy!"

### Alternative Responses

- He's doing great, thanks!
- He's growing up so quickly.
- He's keeping us on our toes!

## 8. Grammar and Natural English Corrections

| Instead of saying | Say this |
|---|---|
| Explain me the process. | Explain the process to me. |
| How the application process work? | How does the application process work? |
| I'll go to evening. | I'll go in the evening. |
| I have got leftover curry. | I've got some leftover curry. |
| I usually do push-ups and cardio exercises. | I usually do push-ups and some cardio. |
| I call my family at the weekend. | I catch up with my family at the weekend. |
