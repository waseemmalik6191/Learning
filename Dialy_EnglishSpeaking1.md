
# Everyday English Speaking Practice

*Practical conversations for work, daily life, family, fitness, and school enquiries.*

---

## How to Practise

Read each section aloud slowly. Then cover the model answer and explain the same idea in your own words.

**Remember:**
- Speak clearly and complete each sentence.
- Practise natural expressions rather than memorising every word.
- Pause when you need time to think instead of filling every gap with "you know".
- Repeat each conversation two or three times.

# 1. Talking About Your Weekend

## Reading Practice

The weather was pretty bad all weekend. It was rainy and windy, so I didn't get outside much.

I caught up on some housework over the weekend and went grocery shopping.

I spent most of my time cooking, doing the laundry, and watching TV. I actually enjoyed cooking.

I've got some leftover curry and roti from last night.

I'm not a big fan of this weather. Hopefully, it'll be sunny tomorrow.

## Common Questions and Answers

**Question: How was your weekend?**

Answer: It was good, thanks. I mostly stayed indoors because of the weather.

**Question: Did you manage to get out?**

Answer: Not really. The weather was pretty bad, so I stayed in and got a few things done around the house.

**Question: What did you do over the weekend?**

Answer: I did some housework, went grocery shopping, and spent some time cooking and relaxing.

### Useful Expressions

| Expression | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Catch up on housework | Complete household tasks |
| Stay indoors | Remain inside |
| Stay in | Not go out |
| Get a few things done | Complete some tasks |
| Leftovers | Food remaining from a previous meal |
| Manage to get out | Find time or an opportunity to go outside |

# 2. Everyday Weather Conversations

## Natural Expressions

Practise these expressions for everyday conversations with colleagues.

- "Morning! Looks like it's lashing rain out there!"
- "It's been raining all weekend."
- "It's absolutely pouring outside."
- "The weather was pretty miserable over the weekend."
- "Hopefully, it'll brighten up tomorrow."
- "I'm hoping for some sunshine."
- "I don't mind the rain, but I'm not a big fan of strong winds."
- "The weather keeps changing."
- "I got caught in the rain and got soaked."

**Irish expression:** "It's lashing rain" means it's raining very heavily. It's commonly used in Ireland.

## Weather Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Chilly | A little cold |
| Windy | With a lot of wind |
| Overcast | Covered with clouds |
| Drizzle | Very light rain |
| Downpour | Very heavy rain |
| Breezy | With a light wind |
| Mild | Not very cold or hot |
| Forecast | A prediction of the weather |

## Short Weather Conversation

**Colleague:** Morning! Looks like it's lashing rain out there!

**You:** It really is. It's been raining all weekend. Hopefully, it'll brighten up tomorrow.

**Colleague:** I hope so. It would be nice to see some sunshine.

**You:** Definitely. I don't mind the rain, but I'm not a big fan of strong winds.

# 3. Talking About the Gym

## Reading Practice

I usually go to the gym in the morning. If I'm busy, I'll go in the evening instead.

I usually do some push-ups and cardio. I try to exercise regularly because it helps me stay fit and feel more energetic.

If I have morning meetings, I sometimes skip the gym and work out later in the day.

## Questions to Practise

1. Do you go to the gym regularly?
2. What kind of exercises do you usually do?
3. Do you prefer working out in the morning or in the evening?
4. How often do you go to the gym?

## Useful Replies

- I try to go a few times a week.
- I usually do some strength training and cardio.
- I prefer mornings, but it depends on my work schedule.
- I try to stay active whenever I get time.
- If I'm busy in the morning, I work out in the evening.

## Useful Vocabulary

| Expression | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Work out | Exercise |
| Strength training | Exercises that build muscle |
| Cardio | Exercises that improve heart and lung fitness |
| Stay fit | Maintain physical fitness |
| Energetic | Feeling active and full of energy |
| Work schedule | Your planned working hours |

# 4. Enquiring About School Enrolment

## Starting the Conversation

"Good morning. I'd like to enquire about enrolling my son. He's three years old."

"Ideally, we'd like him to start as soon as possible."

"Could you please explain how the application process works?"

"Could you please tell me what time the school day starts and finishes?"

"Are there any places available at the moment?"

"Could you please let me know what documents I need to provide?"

"Thank you very much for your help."

## Useful Follow-up Questions

- Is there a waiting list?
- When would he be able to start?
- Are there any fees I should know about?
- Do you offer a school tour or a visit before enrolment?
- What documents do I need to submit?
- Is there an application deadline?

## Role-Play Practice

**You:** Good morning. I'd like to enquire about enrolling my son. He's three years old.

**School Receptionist:** Of course. When would you ideally like him to start?

**You:** Ideally, as soon as possible.

**School Receptionist:** Have you completed the application form?

**You:** Not yet. Could you please explain how the application process works?

**School Receptionist:** Certainly. I'll explain the steps and the documents you'll need.

**You:** Thank you. Could you also tell me what time the school day starts and finishes?

# 5. Talking About Your Family

## Reading Practice

I usually call my family at the weekend to catch up with them.

We talk about how everyone's doing and share what's been happening in our lives.

I enjoy spending time talking to my wife and my little one, even when we're far apart.

## Common Questions and Answers

**Question: How's your family doing?**

Answer: They're doing well, thanks.

**Question: How's your little one?**

Answer: He's doing well, thanks. He keeps me busy!

## Other Natural Replies

- He's growing up so quickly.
- He's full of energy at the moment.
- He's doing great, thanks for asking.
- He's keeping us on our toes!
- He's becoming more active every day.

## Useful Expressions

| Expression | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Catch up with someone | Talk to someone and share updates |
| Little one | An affectionate way to refer to a child |
| Full of energy | Very active |
| Keep me busy | Require a lot of attention |
| On our toes | Keeping us alert and active |

# 8. Grammar Improvements to Remember

Here are some natural English expressions to use in everyday conversations.

| Avoid Saying | Say This Instead |
|---|---|
| I'll go to evening. | If I'm busy, I'll go in the evening. |
| Explain me the process. | Could you explain the process to me? |
| How the process works? | Could you please explain how the process works? |
| He is doing good. | He's doing well, thanks. He keeps me busy! |
| I do push-ups and cardio exercises. | I usually do some push-ups and cardio. |
| I did housework at weekend. | I caught up on some housework over the weekend. |

### Important Grammar Note

**At the weekend** and **over the weekend** are both natural expressions.

- "I usually visit my family at the weekend."
- "I caught up on some housework over the weekend."

"At the weekend" is particularly common in British and Irish English, while "over the weekend" is also widely used.

# 9. Sound More Natural

To improve your spoken English, focus on how you communicate rather than trying to use complicated vocabulary.

## 1. Use Short Sentences

You don't need to explain everything in one long sentence.

Instead of:

"The weather was very bad and I didn't go outside because it was raining and I had a lot of housework to do."

Try:

"The weather was pretty bad. It was raining all weekend, so I mostly stayed indoors. I caught up on some housework."

## 2. Pause Naturally

You don't need to fill every silence with "you know".

Take a short pause when you need time to think.

## 3. Use Natural Expressions

Try using these expressions when they fit the conversation.

| Expression | When to Use It |
|---|---|
| Actually... | Adding information or correcting something |
| To be honest... | Sharing your honest opinion |
| I think... | Giving your opinion |
| For example... | Giving an example |
| Well... | Starting an answer or thinking |
| Anyway... | Moving to another topic or returning to the main point |
| Definitely... | Showing agreement |
| That makes sense. | Showing understanding |

## 4. Practise Speaking Aloud

- Read a paragraph two or three times.
- Record yourself speaking.
- Listen to your pronunciation.
- Repeat sentences that you find difficult.
- Try speaking without looking at the notes.

# 10. Role-Play Practice

## Scenario: Chatting with a Colleague at Work

Imagine you're having a casual conversation with a colleague in the morning.

**Colleague:** Morning! How was your weekend? Did you manage to get out, or was the weather too bad?

**Your Turn:**

Answer in three or four sentences.

Try to mention:

1. The weather.
2. One thing you did.
3. Whether you enjoyed the weekend.

### Example Answer

"It was good, thanks. The weather wasn't great, though. It was raining and windy, so I mostly stayed indoors. I caught up on some housework, went grocery shopping and did a bit of cooking."

## Final Reminder

You don't need perfect English to have a good conversation.

Focus on:
- Speaking clearly.
- Using simple and natural sentences.
- Improving your pronunciation.
- Listening carefully to others.
- Building confidence through daily practice.

**Consistency is more important than perfection. Practise a little every day, and you'll gradually become more confident and fluent.**
